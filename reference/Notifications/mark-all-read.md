---
title: Mark All Notifications as Read
api:
  file: openapi-v0.yaml
  operationId: individualMarkAllNotificationsRead
privacy:
  view: public
---

Marks all notifications as read and clears the unread count.

> This endpoint is shared across Individual and Business Owner user types at the same URL:
> `DELETE /notifications/private/v1/notifications/mark-all-read`
